The standard

What ISO/IEC 17024 means

ISO/IEC 17024 concerns certification of persons. Certification requires authorised schemes, and certification of competence is distinct from work authorisation, visa status, qualification recognition or guaranteed employment.

You will need

  • Identity and employment history
  • Language and trade details
  • Informed consent

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does certification give me a work permit or visa?

No. Work permits, immigration approvals and government recognition are not conferred by platform registration or skills certification.

Does certification guarantee a job?

No. Certification of competence is distinct from work authorisation, visa status, qualification recognition or guaranteed employment.

What does ISO/IEC 17024 cover?

It concerns certification of persons.

Who can issue certification?

Certification services are offered only through authorised schemes and appropriately recognised certification bodies.
