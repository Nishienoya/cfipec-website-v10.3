The journey

From profile to authorised placement

Six stages link worker protection, skills readiness and ISO/IEC 17024 certification pathways. Certification of competence is distinct from work authorisation, visa status, qualification recognition or guaranteed employment.

  1. 1

    Profile

    Identity, employment history, language, trade and informed consent.

  2. 2

    Assess

    Skills evidence and competence gaps; route to qualified assessors.

  3. 3

    Prepare

    Bridging training, workplace readiness, language and safety training.

  4. 4

    Certify

    Assess against an approved persons-certification scheme where available.

  5. 5

    Match

    Shortlist for verified JOBORDERS and check destination-role fit.

  6. 6

    Authorise

    Complete licensed recruitment, contract and immigration approvals.
