Become a Country Partner

Country partners build local worker pipelines and manage approved training, screening and agency relationships. A country-partner designation does not itself constitute a recruitment licence or government appointment.

The role

What a Country Partner does

Country teams translate market demand into a qualified, documented supply pool through approved local channels.

Local sourcing

Use licensed agencies and training networks to identify eligible workers.

Profile registration

Capture consent, identity, occupation, experience, skills and availability.

Skills & readiness

Arrange assessments, bridging training and language preparation where required.

Agency processing

Route matched candidates to the correct local licence-holder and employment process.
