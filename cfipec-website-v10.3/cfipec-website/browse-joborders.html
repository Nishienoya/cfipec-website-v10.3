What Is JOBORDERS?

A community networking platform for people empowerment

JOBORDERS is a community networking platform on people empowerment and enabling manpower monitoring. In line with the International Labour Organization (ILO) standards, Zero Cost Recruitment Policy and comprehensive Foreign Worker Management Policy, we have launched the global JOBORDERS program to support manpower agencies and corporations, and to ensure that they conform to the general principles and operational guidelines.

Employers will be able to verify manpower supply companies that fulfill the international conformity standards with the adoption of ISO 17050 Self Declaration of Services Excellence on Personal Attribution, ethical and code of conduct.

How It Works

How a JOBORDER works

A JOBORDER triggers a controlled recruitment workflow, not immediate deployment.

  1. 1

    Receive

    An employer or trusted receiving partner submits role, volume, location and job terms.

  2. 2

    Verify

    Check employer identity, recruiter status, demand authenticity and relevant legal permissions.

  3. 3

    Standardise

    Translate requirements into role, skill, language, pay, benefits and timeline fields.

  4. 4

    Route

    Allocate the order to eligible country-partner and agency channels and readiness cohorts.

  5. 5

    Monitor

    Track candidate progress, document gaps, approvals, placement and outcomes.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility & requirements

Registered Worker

You must have an active account on the JOBORDERS platform with a complete worker profile.

Valid Certification

Your CFIPEC Global / IPEC Bureau certification must be current and relevant to the JOBORDER's required trade or profession.

Complete CV

A fully built, verified CV is required before your application is visible to employers.

Country Eligibility

Some JOBORDERS have destination-country restrictions. Check each listing for specific requirements.
