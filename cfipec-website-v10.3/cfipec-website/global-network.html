Growing Our Network

CFIPEC Global is establishing strategic partnerships with country organizations to create an integrated international workforce ecosystem. Through these partnerships, we support ethical recruitment practices, workforce development, and cross-border employment opportunities.

Each country partner operates within their local context while connecting to the broader CFIPEC network, enabling workers to access certification, career pathways, and verified international job orders through the JOBORDERS platform.

Current Partners

We are currently partnered with organizations in Cambodia, with additional country partnerships in development across Asia and beyond.

As the network grows, this page will feature detailed information about each country partner, their local initiatives, and the employment opportunities available through their regions.
