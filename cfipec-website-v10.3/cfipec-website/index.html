About

Who We Are

CFIPEC Global is an international workforce organisation established to advance manpower development through strategic market integration, industry engagement, and cross-border workforce collaboration.

Guided by International Labour Organization (ILO) principles and responsible recruitment practices, CFIPEC Global supports ethical workforce development, international talent mobility, and workforce readiness initiatives.

Through its proprietary JOBORDERS initiative and the development of One Stop Centres (OSC), CFIPEC Global aims to strengthen global employment connectivity while promoting upskilling, reskilling, competency development, and sustainable career opportunities.

Vision & Mission

Where we are going, and how we get there

Our Vision

To build an integrated global workforce ecosystem that connects international labour markets, empowers skilled talent, and advances ethical, sustainable workforce mobility.

Our Mission

To strengthen international workforce collaboration through strategic partnerships, responsible recruitment principles, and technology-enabled workforce coordination.

Through JOBORDERS and One Stop Centres (OSC), we aim to support workforce readiness, skills development, international employment opportunities, and long-term career advancement.

How It Works

Five operating stages

A repeatable journey from market demand to worker career continuity.

  1. 1

    Verified JOBORDER

    Confirm employer and vacancies; identify receiving-market requirements.

  2. 2

    Country partner activation

    Dispatch requirements to eligible national partners and agencies.

  3. 3

    Profile, assess, certify

    Build worker readiness and credential evidence, where applicable.

  4. 4

    AI-assisted coordination

    Screen, score, shortlist, document and route to reviewers.

  5. 5

    Lawful placement & repeat

    Authorised deployment, tracking, upskilling and future options.
